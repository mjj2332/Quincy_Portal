3Round 3 — a timeline you can read: 14px labels, one colour per stage, colour by people

Round 2 is beside this one, round 1 beyond it. Taken forward from round 2: the stage-tinted Timeline, the deadline confirmation and drop validation (2c, unchanged), the calendar comboboxes. Changed: every label on the timeline is 14px (meta 12px, nothing smaller); rows are 48px for a project and 40px for a task; each of the five real pipeline stages gets its own treatment, so neighbouring projects no longer read as one beige band; task bars carry the project’s colour as their edge and their progress as the fill; a toolbar switch recolours bars by person. View names follow the shipped Portal: Table, Board, Calendar, Timeline.

3aTimeline, desktop 1440, two-week zoom, coloured by stage. The key row doubles as a stage filter. Hover on “HDR blend — interiors” shows the full task. A label that doesn’t fit its bar sits to its right. Late work gets an oxblood edge and a “late” chip. Press People in the toolbar to recolour by assignee (3b is the same view, drawn that way).
