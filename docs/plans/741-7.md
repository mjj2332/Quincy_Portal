# #741 slice 7 — Version compare (settled plan)

Spec stories 45–51, gate part `compare`. Synthesised from two blind plans (deep-reasoner, Codex) at 82d91fc4.
Where they diverged, the choice and its reason are noted inline. No owner question blocks the slice.

## 0. Decisions
1. **Web-only.** No route, schema, DTO or migration change. `VideoDto.versions[]` already carries each Version's `streamUrl`,
   exact rational fps, `frameCount`, post-rotation `width/height`, `hasAudio` and start TC. `compare` is a UI part reaching the
   web through `review.parts` (already capability-intersected server-side). Streams keep the base video gate; notes keep `notes`.
2. **Two `VideoFrameClock`s + one `CompareTransport` (plain class, `subscribe/getState`).** The master is the audible live side
   (else the other live side; A first when muted). The shared position derives from the master's presented frame, event-driven
   off the clock's rVFC listener. *Codex proposed an independent `performance.now()` clock that corrects by pausing both and
   seeking whenever drift exceeds one frame; rejected because every correction would visibly hitch playback. Kept from Codex:
   drift is measured from presentation timestamps (`mediaTime`), and recovery is bounded.*
3. **Drift (playing):** median of the last three samples. `tol = max(50 ms, 1 follower frame)`. Between `tol` and
   `hard = 0.5 s × max(1, rate)`, the muted follower's `playbackRate` is trimmed ±5% (released below `tol/2`). Above `hard`, the follower is seeked without halting.
   `COMPARE_RATE_TRIM = 0` turns this into seek-only mode as a kill switch. **Paused, stepping or note confirmation:** both sides are
   seeked to exact frames, and the one-frame paused tolerance never accepts a wrong frame (Codex).
4. **Position = A's local frame index `a`** (integer, may lie outside A's range while A is parked). Exact integer mapping in
   `packages/shared/src/video-compare.ts`:
   - `bOf(a) = floor((2a+1)·denA·numB / (2·numA·denB)) − d`
   - `aOf(b) = floor((2(b+d)+1)·denB·numA / (2·numB·denA))`

   Equal rates reduce to `b = a − d`. The domain is `[min(0, aOf(0)), max(nA−1, aOf(nB−1))]`.
5. **Offset `d`:** a signed integer in **B frames**. Positive means B starts `d` B-frames later. Bounds keep ≥1 frame of overlap:
   `[bRaw(0) − (nB−1), bRaw(nA−1)]`. Invalid input is rejected and the bounds are shown. Editing it pauses playback, keeps A still and re-seeks only B. The label reads
   "Offset (frames)" with help text "+12: v2 starts 12 frames after v3". It is stored **per pair** (`${a}:${b}`) in the compare store, and a new pair starts at 0.
6. **Side phases:**
   - `before`: parked on frame 0, "Starts in …".
   - `live`.
   - `last`.
   - `after`: parked on the last frame, "Ended".

   L is the clock's effective `lastFrame()`, never the DTO's `frameCount−1`. A `last` or `after` side is never
   `play()`ed, because the single-player restart-at-end behaviour must not fire. At the shared end, the transport stops. Play from there restarts at the domain start.
7. **Buffering:**
   - **Detection:** any of these counts as a stall:
     - a `waiting` event on a live side;
     - no presented progress on the master for `max(750 ms, 3 frame periods / |rate|)` while it is un-paused (Codex formula, deep floor);
     - a parked side entering `live` with `readyState < 3`.
   - **Freeze and pause:** freeze `a` and pause the *other* side. The stalled side stays un-paused so the browser keeps fetching
     (deep; Safari can stop buffering a paused element). Its stage shows "Buffering v2…" (spinner + `aria-live=polite`).
   - **Resume:** when the stalled side fires `playing`, play the other side.
   - **User Pause:** pauses both and cancels the resume.
   - **Bounded recovery (Codex):** after two failed recoveries within 10 s, stay paused with Notice + Resume.
   - **Hidden document:** pauses both, and returning leaves them paused (Codex; rVFC does not run hidden, so no blind resync).
8. **Safari / gestures:**
   - Every manual `play()` runs synchronously inside the click/keydown handler, on every live side, before any await.
   - The non-gesture `play()`s are stall resume and a parked side entering its window. A `NotAllowedError` from either pauses both
     and shows "Press Play to continue". There is no retry loop.
   - An audio switch flips `muted` inside the gesture through the clock, never through a React prop.
9. **Wipe / layout:**
   - Side-by-side vs wipe is **CSS only**: the same React tree, and the `<video>` elements never remount (identity test).
   - Wipe stacks both cells in one grid area with B on top. B's cell is clipped with `clip-path: inset(0 0 0 Xpx)`, where X is measured across the
     **union of both picture boxes**, so the wipe is over picture rather than letterbox (deep).
   - The handle is the installed `reui/slider` Thumb (keyboard: arrows 1%, Home/End).
   - Name/valuetext: "Wipe between v3 and v2" / "v3 40% · v2 60%".
   - The grip is a 44px coarse target, and pointer-down on the picture jumps the wipe there.
   - Each side has its own `object-contain`, with no stretch or crop. A pair whose aspect ratios differ by more than 1% shows a Notice.
10. **Phones (<721px):** no compare (Lightbox RAW-compare precedent). The Compare button is hidden at `max-[721px]`. Crossing
    below 721px while in compare exits to the single view on A. B is disposed, and focus parks on a connected viewer control.
11. **Audio:**
    - Exactly one audible side.
    - The default is A if it has audio, else B.
    - A Sound ToggleGroup chooses the side; a side without audio is disabled with "No audio".
    - The Mute Toggle covers the audible side.
    - A mode switch never touches audio.
    - Transport-level reverse mutes both and restores them afterwards.
    - On exit, the single player keeps the global mute.
12. **Notes:**
    - There is one notes column with installed `reui/tabs`, one tab per side. Exactly one `VideoNotesPanel` is in the DOM, which avoids
      duplicate fixed IDs (Codex).
    - Both `useVideoNotes` sessions stay mounted, so both marker lanes stay live.
    - The composer, I/O and paste target the active tab's side, at that side's local frame.
    - A note or marker click calls `transport.seekSide(side, frame)`: that side lands exactly, the other side lands by mapping, and the side's tab activates.
    - `awaitConfirmedFrame` on one side triggers the transport's *unexpected stop* rule. That rule pauses and re-aligns the
      **other** side and never seeks the confirming side. Self-issued stops are flagged and never take that path.
13. **Keys:** `usePlayerKeys` (extracted) is bound to the transport.
    - Space, J, K, L, ←, →, Home and End drive both sides. Steps are A-frames.
    - I and O mark the active side, and are unbound while that side is parked.
    - The wipe slider and the offset NumberField keep their own arrows through the existing `playerKeyAction` yield.
    - Compare has no drawing; the 6b Draw control is hidden in compare.
14. **State:**
    - The view mode (`single | compare`) is viewer state.
    - Pair, offsets, mode, wipe, audible side, mute, notes-open and active tab live in `createCompareStore`, held beside `forms` in
      `VideoCollectionPanel`. It uses the same person+Project key and retires with it (lesson "Form lifetime").
    - There is no URL or history entry.
    - Changing one side re-keys only that side's stage. `a` is preserved and clamped, and playback pauses.
    - Entry starts A at the single player's current frame, and exit returns to A's frame.
15. **Escape order** (extends `escapeSnapshot`, taken at keydown):
    1. An open layer.
    2. A note form focused inside a side's panel (`[data-compare-side]`).
    3. Exit compare, with focus going to the Compare button.
    4. Close the viewer.

## 2. Tests
- **7b unit tests:**
  - Equal-rate reduction.
  - Exact 25↔29.97, 23.976↔59.94 and 25↔50 boundaries.
  - Round trip within 1 frame.
  - Bounds guarantee overlap.
  - `driftDecision` bands, hysteresis and kill switch.
- **7b DOM tests (two stubs):**
  - Both `play()` calls happen synchronously, and never on a `last`/`after` side.
  - A paired step is exact.
  - An offset change keeps A still.
  - Master handoff, and the audible switch flips mute synchronously.
  - Trim/hard seek.
  - Stall: B's stall freezes `a`, pauses A, leaves B playing, and resumes on B's `playing`. Pause during a stall pauses both.
  - Bounded recovery.
  - `NotAllowedError` → `blocked`.
  - Unexpected stop never seeks the confirming side.
  - Reverse waits for both sides and stops at the domain start.
  - Dispose removes listeners; hide pauses.
  - Store: per-pair offset, restore after switching back, retire.
- **7c DOM tests:**
  - The gate, a single Version and phone width each hide Compare.
  - Entry and exit frames.
  - `<video>` identity across mode changes.
  - A side change re-keys only that side.
  - Tabs, post anchor, I/O side, two mapped marker lanes, note-click landing.
  - Escape order.
  - Wipe arrows vs Space.
  - Sound disabled without audio.
  - Rate/aspect Notices.
- **Browser pass (7c, Chrome + Safari, 1440/1280/1024 coarse/390):**
  - 60 s at 1x: median |A−B| ≤ 1 frame. Also 20 s at 2x.
  - Slow-4G stall: both freeze and resume together, with no flapping.
  - Safari stall resume without a click.
  - An audible side parked by an offset starts with sound or shows the Notice.
  - Offset nudges against burnt-in frame numbers.
  - Wipe by drag and by keyboard, including a different-aspect pair.
  - Exactly one unmuted element.
  - 1280 with notes open and closed.
  - Phone shows no button.
  - Escape order.
  - Focus rings.
  - Fixture: two burnt-in-frame Versions plus a 29.97 cut.

## 4. Risks
- Safari non-gesture resume and per-element activation.
- The rate trim may stutter on Safari (kill switch).
- Two 4K decoders on older Macs.
- `no-store` doubles range refetches on paired seeks.
- Reverse on long GOPs runs at the slower decoder's pace.
- 1280 with notes open (Notes toggle).

FreeFrame provenance: the transport takes only the *idea* (audible side as master), so it carries no notice.
