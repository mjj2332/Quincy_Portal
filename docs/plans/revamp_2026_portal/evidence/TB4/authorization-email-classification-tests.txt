TB4 authorization / email classification evidence — final state (2026-08-26)

Passed TB4 assertions 7–10 prove from the implementation seam:

  7. Resolver reloads active user/role/capability, current project membership, occurrence
      snapshot intersection, exact mapping (including the mention row's own comment_id, added in
      Sol round 1), project/comment/author visibility, and self guard. The outbox row's own
      schema_version/event_type columns are also validated (added in Sol round 1), not just the
      embedded payload JSON's claims.
  8. Suppression is batched/audited/content-free, acknowledges without retry, and resolver runs
      immediately before in-app and again immediately before email. The suppression ledger update
      and the in-app notification insert are both fenced on the current outbox lease/ownership
      (added in Sol round 1) so a consumer that lost ownership mid-flight can no longer commit a
      notification or suppress a live owner's channel.
  9. Only E_RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED and E_DAILY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED are quota-transient; uncoded errors and
      E_INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR classify as email_acceptance_unknown with no automatic retry. Quota
      delay uses message attempts and bounded delay. `safePayload` now also rejects any
      authorizationAtOccurrence.kind other than exactly "admin" or "project_member" (Sol round 2).
  10. Email failure transitions are pending-only before admission; a processing email is never
      changed to failed/discarded by DLQ, Cron, or Admin discard. **Corrected from the original
      build**: a documented, provably pre-acceptance permanent provider rejection (e.g.
      E_INVALID_TO) correctly resolves to `failed`, not `unknown` — the original build had this
      backwards, contradicting the reviewed plan's §6 step 6; found and fixed by the orchestrating
      session before any Sol review round. Only a genuinely uncoded/unrecognized error, or an
      external observer (DLQ/Cron/Admin discard) that lacks direct proof, stays conservative and
      lands on `unknown`. Admin discard maps a live-processing or already-unknown email to unknown
      and keeps the duplicate warning; it can no longer flip a `suppressed` channel to `discarded`
      (Sol round 2 fix), which would otherwise have made a correctly-suppressed channel replayable
      again.

The pure classifier tests pass quota, internal, uncoded, and permanent-code cases. All legacy
`notifications.email_error` compatibility mirrors (config-missing, send-result, DLQ, Cron,
Admin-discard) are now gated on the guarded ledger UPDATE that produced that outcome actually
having won ownership (`.meta.changes === 1`), rather than firing unconditionally as in the
original build (Sol round 1 fix) — the Cron mirror is additionally bounded and transition-coupled
via a RETURNING clause (Sol round 2 fix).

The Worker integration suite containing live D1/email seam tests
(notification-delivery.integration.test.ts, admin.test.ts) runs and passes outside any codex
sandbox; see automated-gates.txt.
