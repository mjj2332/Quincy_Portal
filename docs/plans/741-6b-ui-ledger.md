## Departure from 6s section 5 ("floating")

6s section 5 floated the Draw button and the drawing pill over the picture. The design review found that the compact pill (326x218) is taller than the picture on a phone (about 191px) and clipped its own tool row, and that the rest Draw button overlapped the timecode chip. Both now dock on the transport strip: Draw is a transport button (before volume / full screen) and the pill replaces the scrubber, controls and key legend in place under the picture while drawing. Reason: nothing covers the picture (only the drawing SVG does), and it matches the owner's preference for the pill under the picture. One breakpoint now governs both the compact pill and the key legend: the `(max-width: 720px)` media query and Tailwind's `max-[721px]` are the same range (720px or less). The drawing stack also takes at least the height the transport block had (measured by `VideoPlayer`), so the picture does not move when Draw is pressed.
