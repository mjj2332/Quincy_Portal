## Traps (lessons)
- Migration ordering above; and every new statement in the archive batch goes **after** the existing ones — `archiveStatementStart = 8` (`projects.ts:1233`) indexes the activity outbox by position (same for the completion batch: activity last).
- Two trust boundaries (lesson, 2026-08-24): part URLs are bearer tokens for 6 h — never logged or audited, response `no-store`; S3 credentials stay on the app Worker; the background sweep uses the R2 binding; the client probe is advisory and only diffed into the audit.
- Presigned PUTs can't cap a part's size; the HEAD size check at completion is the boundary, abort/expiry reclaims parts. Accepted.
- Claim-first R2 deletes; cleanup ownership must outlive the row (hard-delete cascade) — #493/#494 lessons.
- `uploadVideo` checks rerun at completion and abort (role or membership may change mid-upload; External removed → 404, sweep reclaims).
- Hono HEAD→GET; D1 100-bind limit (meta insert ~22 binds, fine); probe ≤64 R2 reads + 3 per completion.
