## 6. Risks and open decisions
- Risks, by lesson: · **"A phone field outside the composer needs its own keyboard layout, and iOS raises the keyboard only for a focus inside the tap":** Add note runs `flushSync(show)`, then a synchronous focus, inside the press; the guest uses `strip.focusField()` the same way. A focus inside a hidden or inert pane silently fails. · **"Safari tints its bars from the page's edges only after the document scrolls":** the immersive edges are ink (bar scrim plus `bg-invert`), and the ink canvas must be on in landscape (S1). · **"`duration-*` without a `transition-*` property animates every property…":** see S1. · **"ItemGroup's sm gap beats a plain gap-0…":** the new row lives inside `ACTION_ROWS`; ActionRow geometry stays on the Item; the hint keeps the 44pt min height. · **"Escape needs an order…":** the snapshot is taken at capture. · **"Tailwind never generates an interpolated arbitrary value":** S3's literal classes.

  1. Immersive composer is the P6 compose strip (recommended: behavioural parity, and the stamp is visible).
  2. Rows: staff after Create Review link; guest after the Download rows.
  3. Landscape with the keyboard up (vv about 150pt): measure in S10. If composer plus dock overflow, hide the dock's control row while the field has focus (D10's "only the composer floats"). Don't pre-build it.
  4. A turn mid-compose enters compose (S5).
  5. The desktop immersive badge stays (settled plan).
