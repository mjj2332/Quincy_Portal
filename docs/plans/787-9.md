# #787 slice 9: public Client Delivery page UI (settled plan)

This plan synthesises two blind plans (Opus deep-reasoner and Codex). PRD §6.7 decides every point where the two disagree. Where this file and `787.md` differ, this file wins for slice 9.

## Route, chunk, fetch
- **Path matcher.** Move the slug test to `@quincy/shared` as `isDeliveryPagePath`. Both the Worker's shell-path check and the SPA use it. It does no decoding, no normalising, and accepts no trailing slash or extra segment. Add `delivery-path.test.ts`, modelled on `guest-path.test.ts`.
- **Mount.** `main.tsx` gets a third branch after the Review and unsubscribe branches and before any staff boot work. It calls `mountDelivery(root, () => import("./delivery/DeliveryApp"))`, a lazy chunk. If the chunk fails to load, show `GuestLoadFailure` with a full reload.
- **API.** The only fetch site is `delivery/delivery-api.ts`. It sends `credentials: "omit"`, ignores aborted or stale responses, strictly parses `deliveryPageDtoSchema`, and returns one of `ok | gone | rateLimited(retryAfter) | transient | outdated`. It also builds the item `media`, `download` and `unlock-request` URLs from the slug and handle.
- **States.**
  - Loading: a skeleton.
  - `gone`: one "This page isn't available." `EmptyState`, the same for unknown, off, unpublished and gate shut. There is no expiry.
  - `transient`: `UnreachableScreen` with retry.
  - `rateLimited`: a countdown from `Retry-After`.
  - `outdated` (the schema did not parse): "This page was updated. Reload."

## Contract amendment
Add `premium: boolean` per item to the public DTO, next to `locked`. Without it the Premium tab can't list items once they are unlocked. Update the strict schema, the builder, fixtures, and the leak and contract tests. Nothing about unlocks or payment is exposed.

## Page (PRD §6.7)
- **Split.**
  - `DeliveryPage` is pure presentation. Props: `dto`, `hrefs` (or `null` in Preview), `onRequestUnlock`, `unlockState`, `downloadAllSlot`. It is sized with container queries, so it works full-page and inside slice 10's 720 px Preview dialog.
  - `DeliveryApp` owns the fetch, the states and the real unlock calls.
  - Slice 10's `DeliveryPreviewDialog` body switches to `DeliveryPage`, with local-only unlock and no downloads.
- **Hero.**
  - The image is the `coverHandle` photo. If it is locked or null, use the first unlocked photo; failing that, a text-only hero.
  - Under it: street and suburb as the single `h1`, "Delivered {date}", and an "Ask the studio" `mailto:` whose subject is the street.
  - The image uses the 2048 `delivery_web` (`url`) with a `srcset` that also lists the thumb, and `fetchpriority="high"`.
- **Tabs** (`reui/tabs`): Photos, Film, Floorplan, Description, and Premium when any item has `premium`. Empty tabs are hidden. Items keep published order, and tab state lives in memory only.
- **Photos.**
  - Grid of thumbs: 4 columns on desktop, 2 at ≤ 721 px. Boxes take their aspect ratio from `width`/`height`, so nothing shifts as images load.
  - Each tile opens the lightbox.
  - Unlocked photos offer **Web-size** (`media`, 2048 JPEG) and **Full-res** (`download`), both as same-origin `download` anchors.
- **Lightbox.**
  - `reui/dialog` full-viewport, holding the base-nova `carousel` (embla), vendored through the sandbox per `docs/reui-reuse.md`.
  - Controls: swipe, ← → keys, Esc, and a live "Photo 7 of 42" counter. Focus returns to the tile on close. Neighbouring slides are preloaded by a hook inside `delivery/`.
  - **Slideshow:** the user starts and pauses it. With `prefers-reduced-motion` there are no transitions.
  - Zoom is deferred (not in PRD).
- **Film.** A native `<video controls playsInline preload="none" poster>`, paused on tab change or unmount, with an error fallback and an original download.
- **Floorplan and Description.** PDF rows (`reui/item`) with View (new tab, `noopener noreferrer`) and Download.
- **Premium.**
  - A locked photo shows `premium_preview`; the watermark is burned in server-side, so there is no overlay.
  - A locked video or PDF has null URLs and gets a labelled placeholder tile.
  - Locked items show a "Premium" badge and "Request unlock", and never a download.
  - A missing derivative shows a processing tile and never falls back to the original.
- **Request unlock.** A POST `{}` per handle. Its state is keyed by handle and survives tab and lightbox changes:
  - sending: disabled
  - 202 `requested` or `already_requested`: "Requested. The studio will be in touch."
  - 409 `not_locked`: refetch the DTO
  - 429: cooldown countdown
  - 404: the gone screen
  - network failure: "Couldn't send. Try again."
- **Download all.** A slot beside the Photos heading renders nothing in slice 9, and nothing probes an endpoint. Slice 7 fills it.
- **Footer.** "Quincy Productions · info@quincyproductions.com.au", from one constant in `delivery/studio.ts`.

## Guards, performance, privacy
- **Boundary guard.** Parameterise `guest-boundary.guard.test.ts` over `{guest/, delivery/}`, each with its own allowed fetch file. Also ban the `/d/api/pages` literal outside `delivery-api.ts`, and ban the staff router, `InternalLink` and `locationStore` in `delivery/`.
- **Vendor chunk.** `vendor-chunk-guard.ts` gets an `EMBLA_MODULE_PATTERN` so embla stays out of the entry and vendor chunks.
- **Images.** Thumbs in the grid, `delivery_web` in the lightbox, 1600 previews for locked photos. `LazyImage` with an explicit public `src`.
- **Privacy.** The server headers stay as they are. The SPA adds `noindex`/`no-referrer` meta tags and sets `document.title` to the street. No storage, no analytics.
- **Accessibility.** Labelled landmarks and tabs, alt text from `label`, controls ≥ 44 px at ≤ 721 px, visible focus, a focus trap with focus return, live regions, and reduced motion.
